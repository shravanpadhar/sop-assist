# SOP Assist: user guide

1. **Sign in** or create an account.
2. **Upload an SOP**: drag a file onto the upload area (PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, Markdown or text; scans and photos work too, up to the size shown). Watch the steps: Uploading, Reading, Understanding, Ready. You can leave the page; progress is saved.
3. **Read the summary** card: purpose, scope, roles, procedures, warnings. Tap a starter question to try it.
4. **Ask**: type a question and press Enter, or tap the round microphone, speak, and it sends when you pause (or tap again to stop). English, Hindi and Hinglish all work; do not choose a language, it is detected for you and the reply follows your question.
5. **Read or listen**: every answer is shown as text. Use the play button to hear it, pause, replay or change speed. Tapping the microphone while it speaks stops the audio at once.
6. **Check the source**: tap a citation chip (section, page, step) to open the exact passage from your document.
7. **Give feedback** with the thumbs, copy an answer, export the chat, or clear the conversation from the toolbar above the chat.
8. **Delete** a document from the sidebar (the trash icon). Its chats are deleted too.

Tips: if the answer is not in the SOP the assistant says so instead of guessing. If the microphone is blocked, allow it in your browser's site settings; typing always works. If you see "The assistant is busy", wait a minute and try again (free-tier limits). Do not upload confidential documents while the privacy notice at the top of the page is showing.
